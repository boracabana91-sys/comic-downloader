CATTYFIED – HOW TO INSTALL
==========================

Easiest way:
  1. Double-click "Double-click me first".
  2. If your Mac says it can't open it: open "Start Here" and follow the pictures.
  3. Wait for "All done! You can close this window."

Prefer typing? Drag Cattyfied onto the Applications folder, then open the
Terminal app, paste this line, and press Return:

  xattr -cr "/Applications/Cattyfied.app"

Then open Cattyfied from your Applications folder.

Why is this needed? Cattyfied is not signed by Apple (that costs a yearly fee),
so your Mac asks you to confirm once that you trust it.
