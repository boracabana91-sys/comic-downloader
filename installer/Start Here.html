If it asks for a password, type the one you use to log in to your Mac. Nothing shows while you type. That's normal. Then press Return.

Did your Mac say it “can't be opened”?

That's normal for apps that aren't signed by Apple. Do this once:

A
In the warning, click Done (or OK). Don't click “Move to Trash”.
⚠️ “Double-click me first” Not Opened
Apple could not verify it is free of malware…

Move to Trash Done
B
Open → System Settings → Privacy & Security. Scroll down to the bottom section called Security.
C
Next to the message about “Double-click me first” (or “Cattyfied”), click Open Anyway. Enter your Mac password if it asks.
Security
“Double-click me first” was blocked to protect your Mac. Open Anyway
D
Go back to the Cattyfied window and double-click “Double-click me first” again. In the next box, click Open. Done!

On older Macs there's a shortcut: hold the Control key, click “Double-click me first”, choose Open, then click Open again.

Prefer typing?

Drag Cattyfied onto Applications. Then open the Terminal app (search “Terminal” with ⌘ + Space), paste this line and press Return:

xattr -cr "/Applications/Cattyfied.app"

Now open Cattyfied from Launchpad or your Applications folder.
